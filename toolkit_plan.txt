Personal Mini-Toolkit Plan

Tools
1. Simple Calculator: Ask for two numbers and an operation, then show the result. It checks for invalid operations and division by zero.
2. To-Do List: Let the user add, view, and remove tasks while the program is running.
3. Number Guessing Game: Pick a random number from 1 to 10 and let the user keep guessing until they get it right.

Exact menu text
Welcome to your Personal Mini-Toolkit!
Choose a tool:
1. Simple Calculator
2. To-Do List
3. Number Guessing Game
4. Quit
Enter your choice (1-4):

Concepts used
- Calculator: input/output, float conversion, conditionals, f-strings.
- To-Do List: input/output, a changing list, loops, conditionals, f-strings.
- Number Guessing Game: random numbers, a loop, conditionals, input/output, f-strings.
- Main menu: loop, if/elif/else, input validation, friendly messages.
